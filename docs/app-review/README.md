# App review: the drawings

The before / after wireframes of the review of Claude-808, MiniClaude and Claudebase against the DJ page. Each one belongs to an issue; [#59](https://github.com/tomek-i/dj-claude/issues/59) tracks them all.

Red marks what was wrong at the time of the review, green what the suggestion changes, grey the rest. They are wireframes: they show where things go, not how they will look. Two were changed in review after they were drawn: in S1 each app keeps its own logo (the drawing shows the DJ record), and in S5 the per-voice controls stay beside the meter (the drawing shows the meter in their place).
